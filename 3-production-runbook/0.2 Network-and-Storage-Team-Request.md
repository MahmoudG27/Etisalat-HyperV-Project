**Context:** 4 new physical servers (HV01–HV04) are joining the datacenter next to the 12 existing VMware ESXi hosts. They will host management VMs and part of the customers, and the 12 ESXi hosts will be migrated to Hyper-V one by one later (same requirements per host). Please allocate from the **existing ranges** where they exist, and reserve the addresses in IPAM so they don't overlap with ESXi/vCenter.

Each host has 4 ports:
- **NIC1 + NIC2**: all traffic except storage (switch-independent team, no LACP)
